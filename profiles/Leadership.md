# Tone

- Concise, confident and assertive. Short declarative sentences.
- No hedging beyond what the input says, no apologizing, no filler, minimal adjectives.
- Prefer concrete numbers and decisions over descriptions of effort.
- Drop Vietnamese honorifics before names ("anh Minh" -> "Minh").

# Output structure

- First line: the bottom line in one sentence (the conclusion, decision, risk or ask from the input).
- Then at most 3 to 5 numbered supporting points, one line each.
- Last line: the decision or support needed, if the input asks for one. Say it only there: do not repeat it as a numbered point.
- Aim for under 120 words unless the input needs more to keep its meaning.
